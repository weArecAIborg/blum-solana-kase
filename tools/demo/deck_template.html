3 · The problem

The bond keeps trading after the record moment, so paying today's holders pays wrong amounts

Kazakhstan's depository pays whoever held the bond at 09:00 on the payment day and does not stop trading. A Solana program cannot list every holder at 09:00.

4 · Record-date mechanism

Each transfer first saves both holders' positions for the record moments that have passed

Token-2022 calls the programThe bond's transfer hook
Save firstBH#458: 12 and BH#43: 5, for record moment 0
Then the bonds moveBH#458: 9, BH#43: 8
Record moment 0123 BH#45812 savednot saved yetnot saved yetnot saved yet

A slot is saved only when the holder next changes. Until then its current quantity is the record-moment quantity, so no snapshot, no freeze, and nobody has to act at 09:00.

5 · Test tokenized instrument

16 · Implemented versus simulated

Everything on Solana is real; only the cash, KYC and the trading venue are simulated

On Solana devnet: implemented
Bond tokenHolder registryPositions at record momentsEntitlementsCoupon vaultsCoupon paymentRedemption with burnBuybackTender offerEvents and accountsServicing by any wallet
Simulated: cash and the issuer's bankA test token minted into the vaults, standing for fiat or a stablecoin
Simulated: KYCThe registrar registers generated wallets, BH#1 to BH#1000
Simulated: the trading venueTrades are direct transfers: no order book, no cash leg

Devnet time is compressed: 1 bond day = 1 to 20 seconds. Limits: fixed-rate bonds, up to 40 coupon periods, direct holders; the registrar and the program's upgrade authority, a single wallet, are trusted.
