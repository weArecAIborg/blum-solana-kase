What runs on Solana, step by step

Every step of the bond's life is one Solana transaction. For each: who may send it, which programs ran in which order (a program calling another is indented under it), every account each one used with its role, what the program checks before it acts, and which balances changed. Each example is a real transaction of the 8-holder devnet run.

1Issue

create_bond

may be sent by: Registrar

The registrar creates the bond in one transaction: the bond token, the bond account that holds the terms and the time table, and the list of accounts the transfer hook needs. The program makes the bond account the token's mint authority, permanent delegate and burn authority, sets itself as the transfer hook, and gives the token no freeze authority.

6Record

record moment: no transaction

may be sent by: Nobody

Nothing is sent at a record moment, and no program runs: a Solana program runs only inside a transaction someone sends. This bond's record moments are 2026-09-07 09:00, 2027-03-09 09:00, 2027-09-06 09:00, 2028-03-06 09:00 (bond time, UTC+5). Each holder record keeps a position history: the quantity now, live_from (how many record moments had passed at its last change) and at[e], the quantity saved for record moment e. The next change to a holding, a transfer, a sale or a redemption, first copies the current quantity into at[] for every record moment passed since live_from. Between two changes a holding is constant, so that copy is exactly the holding at those moments, however late anyone looks.

Measuredrecord moment 0 at chain time 18:26:01 UTC; the first transfer after it (step 7) logged
  • PositionsSaved: BH#1 held 10 at record moment 0
  • PositionsSaved: BH#3 held 0 at record moment 0

A coupon is paid on at_moment(e): at[e] if the holding changed after moment e, else the quantity now. A holder that never trades needs no transaction at all.

source: state.rs

7Trade

transfer (Token-2022) -> transfer_hook

may be sent by: Holder

A holder sends bonds with Token-2022's TransferChecked. Token-2022 moves the balances, then calls bond_servicing's transfer hook with the accounts listed at bond creation. The hook keeps each holder's quantity for every record moment passed since its last change (PositionsSaved), then records the new quantities. This is how the register knows who held what at 09:00 without anyone taking a list.

The rejected part is unlocked. No cash or token account is involved.
