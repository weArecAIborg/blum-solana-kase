The market stays open on coupon day

Kazakhstan's central depository takes the list of bondholders at 09:00 on payment day and keeps trading open. On a blockchain a program cannot see every holder at once, and stopping transfers until the holders are counted would close the market on coupon day. This bond keeps trading open and still pays every holder by the 09:00 list. Every number below is read from the ledger, not from our system.
